# Capstone product narrative

**Goal:** Tell one connected story from discovery through learning.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Discovery evidence
- [ ] Strategy packet
- [ ] PRD and roadmap
- [ ] Prototype/testing
- [ ] Launch and metrics plan
- [ ] Stakeholder/risk plan
- [ ] Post-launch learning loop
- [ ] 90-day next plan

## Acceptance criteria

- Decisions are explicit and traceable to evidence or labeled assumptions.
- Outcomes are defined before features.
- Risks, stakeholders, and unknowns are visible.
- The artifact is understandable without a live presentation.

What would you validate next, and why?
