# 09 — Communication, Stakeholders, and Risk

> **Outcome:** Create alignment and manage uncertainty across teams.

## Checklist

- [ ] Map stakeholders by influence, impact, and information need.
- [ ] Write an alignment/buy-in brief.
- [ ] Practice an active-listening or difficult-conversation script.
- [ ] Create a risk register with qualitative and quantitative assessment, mitigation, and contingency.

## Evidence to keep

Stakeholder map, communication plan, and risk register.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
