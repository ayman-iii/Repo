# 02 — Problem and Product Discovery

> **Outcome:** Find and frame valuable problems before proposing solutions.

## Checklist

- [ ] Run a mind map, brainwriting, SCAMPER, or structured brainstorm.
- [ ] Separate a requested feature from the underlying job or need.
- [ ] Write a problem statement with user, context, pain, outcome, and non-goals.
- [ ] Document assumptions and the riskiest unknown.

## Evidence to keep

Problem brief and discovery decision log.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
