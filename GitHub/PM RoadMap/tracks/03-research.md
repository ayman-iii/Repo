# 03 — Market and User Research

> **Outcome:** Generate trustworthy evidence from people, markets, and competitors.

## Checklist

- [ ] Design behavioral interviews around recent actions and workarounds.
- [ ] Create personas or a B2B role map.
- [ ] Plan surveys or ethnographic observation without leading questions.
- [ ] Compare market, competitor, trend, and customer evidence.

## Evidence to keep

Research plan, notes, synthesis, persona/role map, and opportunity statement.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
