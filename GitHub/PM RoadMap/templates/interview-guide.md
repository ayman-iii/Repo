## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you encountered [problem].
2. What did you do next?
3. What alternatives or workarounds did you try?
4. What was difficult or costly?
5. How did you decide whether the problem was solved?

## Avoid
- Leading questions
- Future hypotheticals
- Asking for reassurance
- Treating opinions as evidence of behavior
