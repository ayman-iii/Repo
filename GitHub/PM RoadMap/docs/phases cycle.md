# PM Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundations and lifecycle

**Learn:** what product management is; product vs. project management; PM roles and responsibilities; key skills; product development lifecycle; introduction, development, growth, maturity, and decline.

**Output:** a one-page explanation of the PM role for a chosen product, including users, customers, business outcomes, and lifecycle stage.

## Phase 1 — Problem and product discovery

**Learn:** idea generation, mind mapping, brainwriting, SCAMPER, brainstorming, problem framing, iterative discovery/selection/validation/execution, and the difference between a request and an underlying need.

**Output:** a problem brief with a clearly framed opportunity, non-goals, assumptions, and initial validation plan.

## Phase 2 — Market and user research

**Learn:** market needs, competitive analysis, emerging trends, personas, behavioral interviews, surveys, questionnaires, ethnography, context, and user/customer/buyer distinctions.

**Output:** research plan, interview guide, evidence table, persona or role map, and opportunity statement.

## Phase 3 — Positioning and strategy

**Learn:** segmentation, USP, positioning, value proposition, Value Proposition Canvas, vision, mission, goals, Five Forces, competitive strategy, partnerships, constraints, and strategic thinking.

**Output:** strategy packet with target segment, positioning statement, value proposition, vision/mission, measurable goals, and trade-offs.

## Phase 4 — Planning and requirements

**Learn:** PRDs, user stories, job stories, feature creep, value vs. features, prioritization, backlog management, story mapping, roadmaps, outcome-based roadmaps, and alignment/buy-in.

**Output:** MVP boundary, PRD, prioritized backlog, roadmap, and decision log.

## Phase 5 — Product design and validation

**Learn:** UX/UI, service and interaction design, design thinking, wireframes, prototypes, usability testing, remote testing, MVPs, and experiment design.

**Output:** prototype, usability plan, findings summary, and prioritized design changes.

## Phase 6 — Agile delivery and launch

**Learn:** Agile, Scrum, Kanban, sprint planning, standups, retrospectives, engineering collaboration, release strategies, feature toggles, phased rollouts, dark launches, go-to-market, and launch planning.

**Output:** delivery plan, release strategy, launch checklist, and stakeholder communication.

## Phase 7 — Metrics and data-informed decisions

**Learn:** North Star Metric, metric trees, DAU/MAU, activation, conversion, retention, churn, LTV, CAC, cohorts, A/B testing, predictive analysis, feedback loops, and showing impact.

**Output:** metric tree, measurement plan, experiment brief, baseline/target/timeframe, and post-launch learning loop.

## Phase 8 — Communication, stakeholders, and risk

**Learn:** active listening, difficult conversations, conflict resolution, influence without authority, stakeholder mapping/engagement, remote stakeholders, risk registers, qualitative/quantitative assessment, mitigation, monitoring, audits, and contingency planning.

**Output:** stakeholder map, alignment brief, risk register, mitigation plan, and escalation path.

## Phase 9 — Advanced practice and leadership

**Learn:** portfolio management, scaling, internationalization, platform thinking, growth strategies, AI/ML in product work, leadership, emotional intelligence, responsible product thinking, and continued learning.

**Output:** capstone narrative and a 90-day development plan.

## Completion definition

You can explain the full product loop, produce the artifacts above, defend prioritization with evidence, define outcomes before features, communicate trade-offs, and identify what remains uncertain.
