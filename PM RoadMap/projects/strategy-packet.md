# Strategy packet

**Goal:** Turn evidence into a coherent strategic choice.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Vision and mission
- [ ] Target segment
- [ ] Positioning and USP
- [ ] Value Proposition Canvas
- [ ] Goals with baseline/target/timeframe
- [ ] Trade-offs

## Acceptance criteria

- Decisions are explicit and traceable to evidence or labeled assumptions.
- Outcomes are defined before features.
- Risks, stakeholders, and unknowns are visible.
- The artifact is understandable without a live presentation.
