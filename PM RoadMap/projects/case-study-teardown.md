# Case-study teardown

**Goal:** Compare product outcomes and identify decision hypotheses.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Context and lifecycle stage
- [ ] User/customer need
- [ ] Market shift or constraint
- [ ] Strategy and execution hypotheses
- [ ] What requires source verification

## Acceptance criteria

- Decisions are explicit and traceable to evidence or labeled assumptions.
- Outcomes are defined before features.
- Risks, stakeholders, and unknowns are visible.
- The artifact is understandable without a live presentation.

## Reflection

What would you validate next, and why?
