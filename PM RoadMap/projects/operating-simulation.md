# Operating simulation

**Goal:** Practice product operations with an engineering-facing team.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Sprint or Kanban plan
- [ ] Standup/review/retro notes
- [ ] Stakeholder alignment brief
- [ ] Risk register
- [ ] Release decision

## Acceptance criteria

- Decisions are explicit and traceable to evidence or labeled assumptions.
- Outcomes are defined before features.
- Risks, stakeholders, and unknowns are visible.
- The artifact is understandable without a live presentation.
