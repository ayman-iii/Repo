# Experiment and launch plan

**Goal:** Connect a hypothesis to measurement and safe rollout.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Hypothesis
- [ ] Primary/supporting metrics
- [ ] Baseline and success threshold
- [ ] Experiment/cohort design
- [ ] Rollout and GTM plan
- [ ] Feedback loop

## Acceptance criteria

- Decisions are explicit and traceable to evidence or labeled assumptions.
- Outcomes are defined before features.
- Risks, stakeholders, and unknowns are visible.
- The artifact is understandable without a live presentation.
