# Product Management Full Guide Map

A GitHub-ready, source-aware Product Management learning map assembled from the supplied roadmap PDF and public links.

## What this repository is

This is a **practical curriculum and portfolio guide**, not a copied course or a claim that every linked resource was independently verified. It turns a broad PM mind map into a navigable sequence of tracks, projects, templates, and case-study prompts.

## Learning arc

1. Foundations and product lifecycle
2. Problem and product discovery
3. Market and user research
4. Positioning and product strategy
5. Planning, requirements, prioritization, and roadmapping
6. Product design and validation
7. Agile delivery and launch
8. Metrics and data-informed decisions
9. Communication, stakeholders, and risk
10. Advanced practice, leadership, and continued learning

## How to use it

- Start with [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md).
- Choose one track at a time and complete its checklist.
- Use the templates to produce portfolio evidence.
- Complete the projects in order; the capstone ties the full loop together.
- Treat tool names as optional examples, not required endorsements.
- Distinguish **source-backed topics** from **authored practice prompts**.

## Source and access notes

The supplied YouTube Studio URL is login-gated, so no private channel data, video titles, transcripts, upload order, or credentials were used. The Canva tracker visibly contained references to internal documents and a credential string; private content and credentials are intentionally excluded. See [`references/sources.md`](references/sources.md).

## Suggested completion standard

For every project, keep: a one-page brief, assumptions/evidence table, decision log, artifact links, metrics definition, stakeholder note, and a short reflection on what you would validate next.
