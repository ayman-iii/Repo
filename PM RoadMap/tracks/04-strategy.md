# 04 — Positioning and Product Strategy

> **Outcome:** Choose whom to serve, why the product matters, and how it will win.

## Checklist

- [ ] Define segment, USP, positioning, and value proposition.
- [ ] Complete a Value Proposition Canvas.
- [ ] Write vision, mission, goals, target, baseline, trend, and timeframe.
- [ ] Record strategic trade-offs, constraints, and partner dependencies.

## Evidence to keep

Strategy packet and assumption/evidence table.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
