# 07 — Agile Delivery and Launch

> **Outcome:** Collaborate with engineering and launch safely.

## Checklist

- [ ] Choose Scrum or Kanban for the context and explain why.
- [ ] Prepare sprint planning, standup, review, and retrospective artifacts.
- [ ] Define rollout, feature-toggle, phased-launch, or dark-launch options.
- [ ] Create a go-to-market and launch checklist.

## Evidence to keep

Delivery plan, release strategy, and launch brief.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
