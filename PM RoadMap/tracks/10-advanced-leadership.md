# 10 — Advanced Practice and Leadership

> **Outcome:** Extend PM judgment into growth, scale, portfolio, AI/ML, and leadership.

## Checklist

- [ ] Analyze platform, internationalization, scaling, or portfolio choices.
- [ ] Evaluate an AI/ML product idea for value, risk, and responsible use.
- [ ] Practice influence without authority and emotional intelligence.
- [ ] Write a 90-day development plan.

## Evidence to keep

Capstone narrative and personal development plan.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
