# 01 — Foundations and Lifecycle

> **Outcome:** Explain PM clearly, distinguish it from project management, and place a product in its lifecycle.

## Checklist

- [ ] Define product management in your own words.
- [ ] Map users, customers, buyers, and business stakeholders.
- [ ] Describe introduction, development, growth, maturity, and decline.
- [ ] Write a role charter: decisions owned, influenced, and escalated.

## Evidence to keep

Role charter, lifecycle diagnosis, and glossary.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
