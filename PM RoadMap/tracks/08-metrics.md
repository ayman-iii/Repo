# 08 — Metrics and Data-Informed Decisions

> **Outcome:** Connect product outcomes to measurable behavior and learning.

## Checklist

- [ ] Define a North Star Metric and supporting metric tree.
- [ ] Specify DAU/MAU, activation, conversion, retention, churn, LTV, and CAC where relevant.
- [ ] Set baseline, target, trend, and timeframe.
- [ ] Design an A/B test, cohort analysis, or feedback loop.

## Evidence to keep

Measurement plan, experiment brief, and learning review.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
