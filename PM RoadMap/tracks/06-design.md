# 06 — Product Design and Validation

> **Outcome:** Use design and testing to reduce usability and solution risk.

## Checklist

- [ ] Sketch a low-fidelity flow or wireframe.
- [ ] Define usability tasks and success criteria.
- [ ] Plan remote or moderated user testing.
- [ ] Turn findings into prioritized design changes.

## Evidence to keep

Prototype, test plan, findings, and iteration summary.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
