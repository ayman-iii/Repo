# 05 — Planning, Requirements, and Roadmaps

> **Outcome:** Translate validated problems into prioritized, outcome-oriented plans.

## Checklist

- [ ] Define MVP scope and non-goals.
- [ ] Write a PRD, user stories, and job stories.
- [ ] Prioritize by value, risk, effort, and learning.
- [ ] Build and communicate an outcome-based roadmap.

## Evidence to keep

PRD, prioritized backlog, roadmap, and decision log.

## Reflection prompts

- What did you learn that changed your decision?
- Which claim is evidence-backed and which is still an assumption?
- What would you do next with more time, access, or data?
