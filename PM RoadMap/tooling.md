# Optional Tool Landscape

The sources name tools by category. They do not establish required proficiency, current pricing, integrations, or endorsements.

| Category | Examples named in sources | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Roadmapping | Productboard, Aha! | Outcomes, initiatives, roadmap communication |
| Planning / project management | Notion, Jira, Linear, Trello | Backlog, issues, planning, documentation |
| Communication | Slack, Teams, Discord | Team and stakeholder communication |
| Analytics | Amplitude, Heap, Looker | Product behavior, reporting, analysis |
| Research / evidence | Google Trends, Google Keyword Planner, annual reports, market-data portals | Demand, trends, market and business context |

Choose tools based on workflow, data governance, accessibility, cost, integrations, and the team's ability to maintain them.
